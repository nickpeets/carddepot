RULES THAT OVERRIDE ANY SCREEN
No image, no entryA card cannot be added and a listing cannot go live without a confirmed front image. The user’s own scan always beats library art.
Stats are pulled truthSeason lines come from records and are read-only everywhere. There is no path where a user types a stat.
Odds are computedPack odds render from the engine at display time. No percentage is ever written into a design or a string.
Placeholders are designedMissing art shows a band with year and name. There is no broken-image state anywhere in the product.
390 is first-classEvery surface is designed at 390 as its own layout, not a squeezed desktop. 44px minimum touch targets.
Locked says whyEvery disabled control carries its reason next to it.
Build order: the four shape parts (button, panel, card tile, stat cell) → nav → binder + spotlight → the Starter Box (a new account needs cards before anything else is testable) → add a card → pack shop + rip → play ball hub → lineup → game. Every screen here is those four parts rearranged.

01

Nav & header

LIVE

The build stacked three rows — wordmark, a boxed MY CLUB card, an email row, then a separate nav row below the header entirely — which is why it reads heavy and confusing. It is one line. Identity, navigation, club status and account all sit in a single 60px navy bar. There is no page title under it — the active nav pill names the surface, and the page’s own controls start one 20px step below.

SPEC
  • One line, 60px tall. D tile · THE DEPOT · Binder · Pack Shop · Play Ball · Marketplace all sit in the same row, reading left to right as one continuous unit. No stacked rows, no second nav, no second Add-a-card.
  • Nav is text, not buttons. Only the active surface gets a gold pill; the rest are plain light-blue labels that tint on hover. Four heavy outlined pills in a row is what made the old bar loud.
  • Club status is understated: record and coins are bare numerals on the navy — VT323 “8-0”, a small “S1”, the spinning coin at 19px, then the balance in gold. No white card, no boxed panel, no “MY CLUB” header. Hairline dividers separate the groups instead of borders.
  • The coin keeps its 4.5s flip but drops to a plain rim at this size — the detailed ring reads as noise below 24px.
  • Email and Log out are 8-bit (Press Start 2P, 7–8px, muted #8fb2c6 / #5f89a8) — the pixel type is what makes an email address feel like part of the game instead of an account setting.
  • + Add a card is the only filled button in the bar, and the only green in the header. It anchors the right end.
  • No page title. The active nav pill is the page label — a surface never repeats its own name below the bar. The page’s first real control row (search + filters) is the first thing under the header.
  • One spacing unit, 20px. Header → search/filter row → content panel all sit exactly 20px apart, so the page reads as evenly stacked bands rather than a header with a gap under it.
  • Why navy: the body is sky blue and card scans are the hero. One dark line frames the page and keeps the coins legible.
  • 390 keeps the line and drops nav to its own strip beneath it; record moves into Play Ball, email and Log out into the account sheet.
States: Signed out — record plate and email drop, wallet reads “—”, account cluster and Add-a-card collapse to one Log in pill, nav pills stay (browsing is public). Loading — wallet shows a shimmer chip, never a zero.

02b

The Starter Box

TARGET

Sits between chapter 02 (log in) and chapter 03 (the binder), and it is the reason a new account has anything to do. On account creation every player opens a one-time 25-card Starter Box — a full club, roster-shaped, mostly plain with one guaranteed bronze-or-better. It is the bridge from “logged in” to “playing games immediately,” and it replaces the empty-binder state.

SPEC
  • A box, not a pack. Bigger, lidded, gold-sealed, with a visible stack of card edges — nothing in the Pack Shop looks like this, because it happens once. Wax packs stay flat and foil; the box has depth and a shadow.
  • 25 = an MLB roster, and it is roster-shaped: a fieldable nine, five starters, five relievers, five bench. Position-aware generation, so a new account can field a legal lineup immediately — that is the whole point of the box.
  • One guaranteed bronze-or-better in the final slot. Everything else is mostly plain. The hit is always last so the sequence has a peak.
  • All library art, all born verified — no no-image placeholders in a Starter Box, ever.
  • Reveal is five waves, not 25 reveals. Infield → outfield → rotation → bullpen → the hit. A wave lands as one beat (cards pop in staggered 0.12s, same cardpop as the rip), then you advance. The 5-card rip pacing does not stretch to 25 — five beats does.
  • Wave progress is a five-dot rail with names, so the shape of the club is legible before it is complete.
  • Skippable at any point — “Skip to the tray” jumps straight to the grouped 25-card tray. The tray is also where the sequence ends naturally.
  • The tray groups by THE NINE / ROTATION / BULLPEN / BENCH + your hit, then offers “Set my lineup” as the primary — the bridge to playing a game.
  • This replaces the empty-binder state. A new account never sees an empty binder; the binder’s first load is 25 real cards plus a gold “your club is filed” bar. Empty-binder now only exists for someone who deletes down to zero.
  • 390 is the same ceremony: box at 230px, waves 2-up with a scroll hint, tray grouped and scrolling. Skip stays reachable on every wave.
States: Mid-reveal (wave n of 5, count n/25, skip always available) · skipped-to-tray (identical tray, no wave rail) · unopened box waiting on the binder (persistent, not dismissible — the box IS the binder’s content until it is opened) · reduced motion (waves cross-fade in, the hit still gets its band and stamp).

LIVE

Live today: catalog autocomplete → season buttons → brand → real card list with art badges → image confirm → ADD. Era/type/key/notes are gone from the form.

SPEC
  • Four gates in order; each one unlocks the next, and the Add button is the last gate.
  • Autocomplete is accent-folded across the 155k-card catalog and shows position + the years cards exist for.

  • Player/pos/team/year/set/# render as a read-only details strip.
States: Locked Add with its reason · checking the catalog · no matches · upload failure · signed out.

  • Buttons lock during the pitch animation; results land as banners plus a play-by-play ticker.
  • Default 3 innings with a 9-inning option.
