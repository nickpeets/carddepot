FRANCHISE ECONOMY — STATIC CLICKABLE MOCKUPS. Baseball-Stars-inspired: cards carry PRESTIGE based on collectible desirability (rookie, star, vintage, rarity). Lineup prestige scales the play-money earned per win. Money spends on PACKS and franchise upgrades — never on stat boosts. Cards always play as their card-year selves; that's sacred.

These wear the existing 8-bit navy/gold shell (shared depot-style.css) with an additive economy.css layer. Hardcoded content, real placeholder names from the collection.
